#  View a car's details

As a User, I want to open a car and see all of its information
So that I can decide whether to schedule a visit or buy it.

The customer needs complete, reliable information before spending time on a visit. Showing the service history and tire condition builds trust in the dealership.

## Acceptance criteria

* The system shows brand, model, year, version, series, mileage, fuel type, transmission, color, tire condition, service history, price and photos.
* The system shows the “Schedule appointment” and “Buy” buttons.
* The system shows a placeholder image when a photo fails to load.
* The system warns the user if the car stopped being available while they were viewing it.
