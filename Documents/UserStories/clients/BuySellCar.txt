As a <client> i need to be able to <buy or sell cars> So that i can <be able to access or determine the price>


Acceptance criteria

- the system will display all of the information directly from the database, showing the correct prices. 

- the system will update the price and stock after any successful registered operation

- the system will prompt the user with the limitations of the price system (no going below X)

- the user will be able to manage his own prices, change and modify it

- the users modifications will be subjected to the systems rules. (interval where price cannot be changed, cooldown and policy's)



completion criteria

- user has completed a buy or sell transaction successfully

- database updates the car stock and price instantly after the operation

- system enforces minimum price limit and blocks invalid price inputs

- user can edit their listing price within allowed system rules and cooldown limits

- system logs price modifications and applies defined policies and restrictions
